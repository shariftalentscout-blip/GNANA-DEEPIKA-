My Story

Learning Never Stops.

From learning through experience to sharing knowledge with others.

My name is Shaik Sharif. I am from Bhimavaram and I completed my MCA.

After completing my education, I worked in different environments, including the banking sector and Flipkart.

While working, I realized that education should not stop with completing a degree. I started continuously learning new skills, technologies, tools and practical concepts to move forward.

That learning journey eventually led me to start SS CAREER HUB.

I divided my work into three major areas: Job Seekers, Schools, and Small Businesses. The objective is to create practical support, projects and digital solutions for these communities.

Whatever I have learned, I wanted to share it with others. Gnana Deepika is my attempt to bring that knowledge, experience and learning mindset into one platform.
